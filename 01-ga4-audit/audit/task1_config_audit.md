## My 3 most business-critical findings
*Ranked with a Reach × Money × Ease score (1–3 each). Scores in brackets.*

1. **Key events count browsing as conversions (check 9) [Reach 2 · Money 3 · Ease 3 = 8].** `view_item` and an undocumented test event `xyz` are marked as key events, so "conversions" are dominated by product views (~70× purchases). **Why it matters:** with Google Ads linked, automated bidding can spend budget chasing browsers instead of buyers, and key event rate is meaningless. **Fix:** unmark both (one toggle, 5 minutes), keep `purchase` primary and `add_to_cart` secondary.
2. **Internal traffic is invisible (check 2) [3 · 2 · 2 = 7].** Both filters are in Testing, and the impact test shows they catch **0%** of traffic, while signs of internal visitors exist (e.g. `art-analytics.appspot.com`, 2,998 sessions). **Why it matters:** staff sit inside every customer KPI (users, sessions, conversion rate, revenue) by an amount nobody can measure. **Fix:** define the internal-traffic rule, flag employee orders with a user property, segment rather than delete.
3. **Consent mode incomplete (check 12) [3 · 2 · 1 = 6].** Analytics and Ads cookie consent signals are inactive. **Why it matters:** visitors who refuse cookies are lost or tracked without valid consent, a data-completeness gap and a **GDPR risk** for EU traffic. **Fix:** Consent Mode v2 with all 4 signals via the CMP (needs dev + legal).

*Also scored 6, just outside the top 3:* refunds not tracked (check 6, revenue overstated ~$15–37k, assumption) and Unassigned 5.78% with UTM errors (check 11).
